Vibe-coded games, in your pocket.

PocketVibe is a launcher and game store for ROCKNIX and Android handhelds, full of three.js games made with AI coding tools. Pick one and play it with the handheld's buttons.

    What you need

    Linux Android
    System ROCKNIX. Not muOS or other firmware Android 10 or newer, with Android System WebView 94 or newer
    Hardware A 64-bit Arm chip such as the H700 or RK3566, 1 GB of RAM or more, and the Panfrost graphics driver (PocketVibe offers to switch from libmali) A handheld with built-in buttons
    Free space About 1 GB on the SD card About 50 MB
    Internet Wi-Fi, for the first start (150 MB, once) and the store Wi-Fi, for the store
    Tested on Anbernic RG34XX SP, RG34XX and the two-screen RG DS Anbernic RG Rotate

    Other ROCKNIX handhelds with the same chips, such as the RG35XX and RG40XX families, should work too, but are not tested yet.

    Put it on your handheld

    On Linux (ROCKNIX)

    PocketVibe.zip newest version
    1. Turn on Samba in ROCKNIX's network settings, open the handheld's games-roms share from your computer and unzip PocketVibe.zip into ports.
    2. Restart the handheld, or update its game lists, then open Ports and PocketVibe.
    3. The first start downloads the game engine, about 150 MB, in a minute and a half. Then PocketVibe opens with five games.

    Leave a game: hold Start + Select.

    On Android

    PocketVibe.apk newest version
    1. Open the download on the handheld. When Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps.
    2. Tap Install, then start PocketVibe from the app list.
    3. It opens with five games, and installs new versions over itself from Settings.

    Leave a game: hold Start + Select, or press Back.

    Using it

    L R
    Library, Store and Settings
    A
    Play, or download in the Store
    B
    Back; on the tabs, quit
    Y
    Sort the games
    X
    Store: hide the games you have
    Select
    Cards or a list

    The bar along the bottom of the launcher always shows what each button does. PocketVibe checks for new versions of itself and offers them in Settings > App update.
