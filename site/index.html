Vibe-coded games, in your pocket.

PocketVibe is a launcher and game store for ROCKNIX and Android handhelds, full of three.js games made with AI coding tools. Pick one and play it with the handheld's buttons.

    What you need

    PocketVibe runs on ROCKNIX and Android handhelds with buttons.

    ROCKNIX handhelds

    System
    ROCKNIX. PocketVibe does not run on muOS or other firmware.
    Chip
    64-bit Arm, such as the Allwinner H700 or the Rockchip RK3566
    Memory
    1 GB of RAM or more
    Graphics
    The Panfrost driver. On libmali, PocketVibe offers to switch.
    Storage
    About 1 GB free on the SD card
    Network
    Wi-Fi, for the first start (150 MB, once) and the store

    Android handhelds

    System
    Android 10 or newer
    Web engine
    Android System WebView 94 or newer, updated from the Play Store
    Controls
    Built-in buttons: a d-pad, A, B, X, Y, L, R, Start and Select
    Network
    Wi-Fi, for the store

    Tested on

    • Anbernic RG34XX SPROCKNIX, H700
    • Anbernic RG34XXROCKNIX, H700
    • Anbernic RG DSROCKNIX, RK3566, two screens
    • Anbernic RG RotateAndroid

    Other ROCKNIX handhelds with the same chips, such as the RG35XX and RG40XX families, should work too, but are not tested yet.

    Put it on your handheld

    A PocketVibe for ROCKNIX PocketVibe.zip · newest version B PocketVibe for Android PocketVibe.apk · newest version

    On ROCKNIX

    1. 1

      Download PocketVibe.zip

      5 MB. The handheld needs ROCKNIX, Wi-Fi and about 1 GB free on its SD card (what you need).

    2. 2

      Unzip it into roms/ports

      Over the network: turn on Samba in ROCKNIX's network settings, then open the handheld's games-roms share from your computer. A second SD card for games can also be filled straight from your computer.

    3. 3

      Restart, then open Ports and PocketVibe

      The first start downloads and installs the game engine, about 150 MB, in about a minute and a half over Wi-Fi. Then PocketVibe opens with five games, in seconds from then on, and keeps itself up to date.

    In a game, hold Start and Select to go back to PocketVibe.

    On Android

    1. 1

      Download PocketVibe.apk

      On the handheld. It needs Android 10 or newer.

    2. 2

      Open it and tap Install

      When Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps.

    3. 3

      Start PocketVibe from the app list

      Made for handhelds with buttons, such as the Anbernic RG Rotate. A new version installs over the old one, and PocketVibe updates itself from Settings.

    In a game, hold Start and Select, or press Back, to go back to PocketVibe.

    Using it

    L R
    Switch between Library, Store and Settings
    A
    Play a game, or open a game's page in the Store, where A downloads it
    B
    Go back; on the tabs, quit PocketVibe
    Y
    Sort: recently played, newest, most downloaded, A to Z or by category
    X
    In the Store, hide the games you already have
    Select
    Cards or a list
    Start + Select
    Held in a game: back to PocketVibe. Held for three seconds: quit

    The bar along the bottom always shows what each button does. PocketVibe checks for a new version of itself every few hours and offers it in Settings > App update.
