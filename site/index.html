Vibe-coded games, in your pocket.

PocketVibe is a launcher and game store for ROCKNIX and Android handhelds, full of three.js games made with AI coding tools. Pick one and play it with the handheld's buttons.

    Put it on your handheld

    Free, with no account. On ROCKNIX it takes a couple of minutes, on Android one.

    Linux handhelds

    with ROCKNIX

    Download PocketVibe.zip newest version
    1. Turn on Samba in ROCKNIX's network settings, open the handheld's games-roms share from your computer and unzip PocketVibe.zip into ports.
    2. Restart the handheld, or update its game lists, and open Ports, then PocketVibe.
    3. The first start sets up the game engine (150 MB, about a minute and a half), then opens with five games.
    Needs
    ROCKNIX (not muOS or other firmware), a 64-bit Arm chip such as the H700 or RK3566, 1 GB of RAM, the Panfrost graphics driver, 1 GB free and Wi-Fi
    Tested on
    Anbernic RG34XX SP, RG34XX and RG DS. Other ROCKNIX handhelds with these chips should work too.

    Android handhelds

    Android 10 or newer

    Download PocketVibe.apk newest version
    1. Open the download on the handheld. When Android asks, let your browser or file manager install apps.
    2. Tap Install, then start PocketVibe from the app list.
    3. It opens with five games, and updates itself from Settings.
    Needs
    Built-in buttons, Android System WebView 94 or newer, about 50 MB free and Wi-Fi
    Tested on
    Anbernic RG Rotate

    Make your own game

    Describe a game to your AI coding tool and play it on your handheld the same day. Our brief teaches it the handheld: the limits we measured, the rules that keep a game at 60 fps, and how to try it and publish it.

    AHow to make a game BThe brief for your AI 
    # Start a game
npm create pocketvibe@latest my-game

# Tell your AI tool
Read https://pocketvibe.cobanov.dev/agents.md
and follow it. Then make me a snowboard race.

# Play it on your handheld
npx pocketvibe serve

# Send it to the store
npx pocketvibe publish
