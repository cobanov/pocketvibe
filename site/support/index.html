Support

Something not working, a question, or a game that should not be in the store? Write to support@pocketvibe.dev. We answer within a few days.

Playing

  • Getting games: the Store tab lists every game; open one and press A to download it. It then sits in your Library.
  • Updates: a game with a newer version in the store shows a badge, and A updates it before it starts. Settings, Update all games, gets them all.
  • Leaving a game: hold Start and Select together.
  • Removing a game: open it in the Library with X, then press Y.

Controls on a phone

A phone gets the handheld's buttons on its screen: the d-pad, A, B, X, Y, L, R, Start and Select. Upright, the game sits at the top and the buttons below it; on its side, the buttons sit either side of it. Connect a gamepad and the buttons on the screen make way for it.

No sound on the iPhone?

The games follow the ring/silent switch: turn silent mode off. The sound starts with the first button you press.

Reporting a game

Every game in the store is open source and reviewed before it is published. If one is offensive, broken or copies someone else's work, open its page, press X (More) and choose Report this game. Reports go to the store's maintainer, who looks at them within a few days and removes games that break the rules. Hide games by in the same menu keeps that author's games out of your store.
