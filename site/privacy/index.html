Privacy

PocketVibe collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, in the apps or in the games.

What stays on your device

Your games, your saves and high scores, your settings and the games you played last are kept on the device and never sent anywhere. Removing the app removes them.

What the app asks the internet for

  • The store's list of games, the games and their covers, from the stores in Settings (the PocketVibe Store by default). The store counts how many times each game is downloaded; it does not know or keep who downloaded it.
  • App updates on Android and the handhelds, from GitHub. On the iPhone, updates come from the App Store or TestFlight.

Like any web server, the store and GitHub see the network address a request comes from; the store does not log or keep it.

Reports

When you report a game (a game's page, More, Report this game), the store keeps the game, what you chose as the problem and which kind of device sent it (for example "ios"). Nothing about you is sent or kept.

Children

PocketVibe collects nothing from anyone, children included. On the iPhone the store lists only games rated for the app's age rating.
