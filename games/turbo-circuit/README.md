# Turbo Circuit

An arcade lap racer for handheld consoles running ROCKNIX (first target: Anbernic RG SP), built on the PocketVibe template.

Four circuits, each with its own layout, scenery and colors: **Sunny Park** (flowing curves and one tight hairpin), **Canyon Run** (a desert dash down a huge straight into a hairpin), **Maple Woods** (twisty and technical, in misty autumn woods) and **Frost Peak** (fast sweepers and a deep hairpin in the snow). Choose one on the title screen with the D-pad; its best race and best lap are saved.

Three laps against three rival cars. You start at the back of the grid; press A on "1" of the 3-2-1-GO countdown and hold it for a turbo start. Hold A to accelerate, brake into the hairpins with B, cut across the curbs and hit the glowing boost pads for a turbo burst. The grass slows you down, cars bump and spin each other, cones go flying. Rivals follow their own racing line at slightly different paces, and ease off or push harder depending on how far ahead or behind you they are. Once the winner crosses the line you have 40 seconds to finish. Every lap time pops up as you cross the line, and the results show the final order, your time and any new records.

Sound effects for everything on the track (an engine that climbs through its gears, the rivals' engines, tyre squeal, curbs, grass, bumps, cones, boost pads, the countdown, laps and finish jingles) and music on the title and during the race. Sound and music can each be switched off in Options on the title screen or in the pause menu.

On a handheld with two screens the second one shows a large map of the circuit, the standings, your speed and best lap.

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| A | Accelerate (menus: select) |
| B | Brake, then reverse; brake while steering to slide (menus: back) |
| D-pad left / right | Steer (title: choose the circuit) |
| D-pad up / down | Menus: move |
| START | Pause menu: resume, sound, music, quit to title (paused: START or B resumes) |

Code: `src/main.js` (states, menus, chase camera and loop), `src/circuits.js` (the four circuits: control points, boost pads, laps, colors, scenery), `src/world.js` (builds every circuit while loading and switches between them), `src/track.js` (the spline circuit, lap tracking, AI racing line, road and boost pad meshes), `src/bake.js` (static scenery merged and cut into cells, so only the cells in view are drawn), `src/car.js` (arcade car physics, AI driver, car-to-car bumps, car model), `src/race.js` (grid, standings, lap times, effects), `src/scenery.js` (sky, arch, grandstand, boards, trees, cacti and rocks, knockable cones), `src/fx.js` (pooled particles), `src/hud.js` (position, lap, time, speed, minimap, standings, banners), `src/audio.js` (engine, tyre and surface sounds), `src/shared.js` (constants and helpers). `src/handheld.js` is the unchanged device layer from the template, `src/sound.js` the shared PocketVibe sound module. The effects in `public/sfx/` are generated by `tools/sfx/games/turbo-circuit.py` in the PocketVibe repository; the music is `public/music/theme.ogg`.

## Start

```sh
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open the address it prints. The game shows in a 720×480 frame, the size of the handheld's screen.

## Controls in the browser

| Handheld | Keyboard |
|---|---|
| D-pad | Arrow keys |
| A | X |
| B | Z |
| X | S |
| Y | A |
| L / R | Q / W |
| Start | Enter |
| Select | Shift |

`P` toggles the performance overlay (fps, draw calls, triangles). It turns red when the game is too heavy for the handheld.
