# Block Drop

A falling-block puzzle for handheld consoles running ROCKNIX (first target: Anbernic RG SP), built on the PocketVibe template.

Seven kinds of pieces fall into a 10×20 well of chunky bevelled 3D blocks. Slide and rotate them so they fill whole rows: a full row flashes and bursts, and everything above drops down. Pieces come from a 7-bag (every seven pieces hold one of each), rotate with SRS wall kicks, and wait half a second on the stack before locking, so there is time for a last slide or spin. A ghost shows where the piece will land, the next three wait on the right and one piece can be kept in the hold box. Every press acts on the frame it arrives; holding LEFT or RIGHT slides the piece after 0.15 s, a column every other frame, and presses made while rows clear (or before GO) are kept for the next piece.

Clearing 1, 2, 3 or 4 rows scores 100, 300, 500 or 800 points times the level. Turning a T into a tight slot as its last move is a T-spin (three of the four corners around its centre filled) and scores more: 400 for the spin alone, 800, 1200 or 1600 with 1, 2 or 3 rows (a mini T-spin, with one front corner open, scores less). Quads and T-spins in a row earn a back-to-back bonus, clears in a row a combo bonus, and emptying the whole well an all clear bonus. Soft drops score 1 point per row, hard drops 2. The grid glows red and a heartbeat sounds as the stack nears the top.

Three modes, picked on the title screen:

- **Marathon**: endless. Every 10 lines the level goes up, the pieces fall faster (the usual guideline curve, a row a second at level 1) and the sky changes colour.
- **Sprint**: clear 40 lines as fast as you can, at level 1 speed. The sky moves on every 10 lines.
- **Dig**: the well starts with 10 rows of grey garbage, each with one hole; dig them all out against the clock.

Marathon keeps your best score, Sprint and Dig your best time. Sound effects and energetic electronic music play throughout; the title's Options and the pause menu turn sound effects and music on or off.

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| D-pad left / right | Move (hold to slide across) |
| DOWN | Soft drop; down in menus |
| UP | Hard drop; up in menus |
| A | Rotate clockwise; pick a menu entry; play again |
| B | Rotate counter-clockwise; back in menus, resume; title from the results |
| L or R | Hold the piece (once per piece) |
| START | Pause menu / resume |

Code: `src/main.js` (states, menus, modes, sounds, camera and the loop), `src/game.js` (rules: movement with auto-repeat, rotation, T-spins, lock delay, line clears, scoring, the modes), `src/pieces.js` (shapes and SRS kick tables), `src/well.js` (well, side panels, every block as one InstancedMesh, ghost piece), `src/fx.js` (particles), `src/backdrop.js` (sky and drifting blocks), `src/hud.js`, `src/shared.js` (constants and geometry helpers). `src/handheld.js` is the unchanged device layer from the template and `src/sound.js` the shared PocketVibe sound module. The effects in `public/sfx/` are generated by `tools/sfx/games/block-drop.py` in the PocketVibe repository; the music is `public/music/theme.ogg`.

## Start

```sh
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open the address it prints. The game shows in a 720×480 frame, the size of the handheld's screen; the links under it try the other screen shapes.

## Controls in the browser

| Handheld | Keyboard |
|---|---|
| D-pad | Arrow keys |
| A | X |
| B | Z |
| X | S |
| Y | A |
| L / R | Q / W |
| Start | Enter |
| Select | Shift |

`P` toggles the performance overlay (fps, draw calls, triangles). It turns red when the game is too heavy for the handheld.
