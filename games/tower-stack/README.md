# Tower Stack

A stacking arcade game for handheld consoles running ROCKNIX (first target: Anbernic RG SP), built on the PocketVibe template.

A slab slides back and forth over the top of the tower, along one axis and then the other, and A drops it. Whatever hangs over the edge is sliced off and tumbles away, so the next slab is only as big as what is left; drop it within a hair of the layer below and it snaps perfectly into place with a ring of light and a chime. Perfect drops in a row build a streak: the chime climbs the scale, every fifth one in a row bursts in gold, and from the third one on each perfect drop grows the slab back a little towards its starting size (the sliding slab glows while the next perfect drop will grow it). Your score is the number of layers. Miss the tower completely and the run is over: the camera pulls back to show the whole tower (A skips ahead to the results).

The slab slides a little faster with every layer, easing towards a top speed instead of speeding up forever, and the perfect window never gets shorter than 40 ms either side of the centre. From the eighth layer it sometimes comes in from the other side. Every 25 layers the sky moves on: golden hour, sunset, a starry night with shooting stars, midnight, dawn and a new day. While you climb, a gold line marks the height of your best tower; the best height and the sky it reached show on the title and results screens.

Sound effects, a wind that grows with the height and calm music play throughout; the title's Options and the pause menu turn sound effects and music on or off.

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| A / UP | Drop the slab; play again |
| D-pad UP / DOWN, A | Choose and pick in the title and pause menus (LEFT / RIGHT also flip On / Off) |
| START | Pause menu / resume |
| B | Back in menus, resume; title from game over |

Code: `src/main.js` (states, menus, scoring, sounds, camera and the title demo), `src/stack.js` (the sliding slab and the drop: slicing, perfect snaps, growing back), `src/tower.js` (the placed layers, one InstancedMesh), `src/debris.js` (falling offcuts), `src/fx.js` (rings and sparkles), `src/sky.js` (sky gradient, stars, shooting stars, sun and moon, the sky milestones), `src/clouds.js` (cloud bank and drifting clouds), `src/hud.js`, `src/shared.js` (constants, colours and the slab geometry). `src/handheld.js` is the unchanged device layer from the template and `src/sound.js` the shared PocketVibe sound module. The effects in `public/sfx/` are generated by `tools/sfx/games/tower-stack.py` in the PocketVibe repository; the music is `public/music/theme.ogg`.
